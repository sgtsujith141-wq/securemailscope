## Why it was superseded

It was correct and honest, but it was still built to be read rather than
presented. Judged against a projector at several metres:

- body text sat at 8 to 9 point across most panels, which is unreadable from
  the back of a hall;
- the proof of the whole idea -- a finding and the packets behind it -- was a
  screenshot small enough to read as a dark rectangle;
- slide 5 carried three separate screenshots where one large one and two
  supporting crops would say more;
- the references slide listed twenty numbered items, which is a bibliography
  rather than a closing statement.

The replacement keeps the same content and the same claims, and rebuilds the
layout for a room: larger type, fewer and larger visuals, and a four-step
observe-trace-assess-remediate graphic in place of the paragraph that used to
explain it.
